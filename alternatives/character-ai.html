About Character.ai

💰 $9.99/mo (c.ai+)

Character.ai is a conversational AI platform for creating and chatting with custom AI characters and personas. Hugely popular for entertainment, roleplay, and social interaction.

  • Free tier has wait times during peak hours
  • $9.99/month for priority access
  • Characters are stored on Character.ai servers
  • Content filtering limits certain types of roleplay
Why look for Character.ai open source alternatives? Self-hosted character chat solutions give you full control over persona settings, no content filtering, and the ability to run any open model locally.

Frequently Asked Questions About Character.ai Alternatives

What is the best free alternative to Character.ai?

SillyTavern is the most feature-rich free alternative, offering advanced character cards, lorebooks, world info, and compatibility with virtually any local or API-based model. It has a large community of custom character creators.

Is there a Character.ai alternative with no content filter?

Self-hosted solutions like SillyTavern + KoboldCpp with local models have no external content filtering. Users are responsible for their own usage and must comply with model licenses.

Can I create custom AI characters for free?

Yes. SillyTavern supports 'character cards' — JSON files defining a character's personality, scenario, and example dialogue. Thousands of community-created character cards are freely available online.

What models work best for character roleplay?

Models fine-tuned for roleplay work best: MythoMax, Noromaid, or various community RP finetunes running in KoboldCpp or Ollama. Llama 3 70B base models also perform well for character consistency.
