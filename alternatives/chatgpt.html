ChatGPT Alternatives in 2026

Find the best open source ChatGPT alternatives — self-hosted, free, and private replacements that give you control over your data and costs.

About ChatGPT

💰 $20/mo (Plus)

The world's most popular AI chatbot by OpenAI, known for its conversational ability, coding help, and broad general knowledge. The free tier is limited; ChatGPT Plus at $20/month unlocks GPT-4o.

  • Requires monthly subscription for GPT-4 access
  • All conversations sent to OpenAI servers
  • No self-hosting option
  • Usage limits even on paid plan
Why look for ChatGPT open source alternatives? If you want privacy, unlimited usage, or the ability to run your own models, these open source ChatGPT alternatives give you a web chat UI you can host yourself — often backed by any model you choose.

Frequently Asked Questions About ChatGPT Alternatives

Can I run ChatGPT locally for free?

ChatGPT itself cannot be run locally — it's a proprietary service. However, you can run open source alternatives like Open WebUI or LibreChat paired with Ollama to get a similar experience completely free and offline.

What is the best free alternative to ChatGPT?

Open WebUI + Ollama is the most popular combination, providing a ChatGPT-like interface that runs locally for free. For a hosted solution with multiple model providers, LibreChat is the top choice.

Is there a private alternative to ChatGPT?

Yes. Jan, Open WebUI, and PrivateGPT all run entirely locally — your conversations never leave your machine. These are ideal for sensitive work or users in regions with data sovereignty requirements.

Which ChatGPT alternative supports the most AI models?

LibreChat supports over 30 providers including OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Mistral, and local Ollama models. ChatALL lets you query multiple models simultaneously to compare responses.
