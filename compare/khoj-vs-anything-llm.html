Khoj VS AnythingLLM

Khoj vs AnythingLLM

Khoj and AnythingLLM are both personal AI assistants that let you chat with your documents and notes. Khoj focuses on being your personal AI second brain — it indexes your notes, documents, and calendar to answer questions about your own knowledge. AnythingLLM is more of a general-purpose private AI workspace for teams and individuals. Khoj is more personal-assistant focused; AnythingLLM is more enterprise-workspace focused.

🗓 Updated: 2026-06 ⭐ Khoj: 35k+ stars ⭐ AnythingLLM: 62k+ stars

⚡ TL;DR — 30-Second Verdict

Choose Khoj if you want an AI assistant that understands your personal knowledge base — Obsidian notes, Org files, PDFs — and helps you think. Choose AnythingLLM if you need a full-featured AI workspace for a team with document management, multi-user support, and flexibility to swap LLMs. Khoj is your personal second brain; AnythingLLM is a team AI platform.

What Is Khoj?

Khoj's 16k+ community validates its utility—this isn't a weekend project, it's maintained software. Useful for teams building internal knowledge assistants. The main consideration is chunking strategy—the default settings work for getting started, but production quality requires tuning chunk size and overlap for your specific document types.

What Is AnythingLLM?

AnythingLLM has found solid traction with 26k+ GitHub stars, indicating real-world adoption beyond early adopters. A practical choice for document Q&A and knowledge base applications. The RAG pipeline abstractions save significant engineering time compared to rolling your own chunking and retrieval logic. For production use, plan for careful index management as document collections grow.
