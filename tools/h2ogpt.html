What Is h2oGPT?

h2oGPT is an open-source end-user AI application with 11k+ GitHub stars. Private enterprise-grade Q&A with local document retrieval

As a end-user AI application, h2oGPT is designed to help developers and teams integrate AI capabilities into their projects without building everything from scratch. It provides a ready-to-use interface that reduces the time from idea to working prototype.

The project is maintained on GitHub at github.com/h2oai/h2ogpt and is actively developed with a strong open-source community. With 11k+ stars, it is one of the most widely adopted tools in its category.

  • 🏠
