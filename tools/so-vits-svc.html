Frequently Asked Questions 常见问题

What is so-vits-svc? ▼
so-vits-svc is a singing voice conversion tool that uses VITS and SoftVC techniques to convert the timbre of one voice to another while preserving pitch and rhythm. It's widely used for creating covers of songs in different voices.
Is so-vits-svc legal to use? ▼
The software itself is MIT licensed. However, cloning artists' voices without permission raises significant legal and ethical concerns. Many voice cloning uses may infringe on performers' rights. Only use voice cloning with explicit consent from voice owners.
How does so-vits-svc compare to RVC? ▼
Both are voice conversion tools, but RVC (Retrieval-based Voice Conversion) has become more popular due to its faster training and better community tooling. so-vits-svc may produce higher quality in some cases but requires more resources.
